One question, five answers: uninsured deposits at a fintech becoming a bank

A synthetic startup-banking fintech with partner banks, a $5M sweep and a mid-history migration off a legacy partner. 12,000 business depositors, 25,373 accounts, seven quarter ends. Staging and mart models with ref(), four tests, and a schema.yml that states every model's grain. Runs in about 3 seconds on the Python standard library.

02

The sweep allocates by account holder; the FDIC insures by owner

A sole proprietorship is insured as its owner, together with the owner's personal accounts at the same bank. The sweep engine treats the business and the person as two holders. Each keeps its own $250K at the partner and fills the same program banks first.

05

The grain that's right for insurance is wrong for concentration

GrainTop 10 holdLargest single key
Insured owner6.5%1.2%
Founder6.5%1.2%
Lead investor's portfolio39.0%14.4%

The size of the investor figure comes from my assumption about how concentrated lead investors are (a Zipf curve over 400 of them), so read it as a shape, not a forecast. The shape holds at any setting: no customer-level grain can show that one investor's portfolio may move its cash on the same day.

The part I got wrong

I first built concentration on the founder, on the theory that one person behind several entities is "one customer." It moved the top-10 share from 6.5% to 6.5%. Founders rarely run more than one cash-heavy company at once. The grain that moves together is the investor, which is what the 2023 bank runs looked like, and it only showed up once I modeled it.

06

The contract an AI analyst reads

Every model states its grain, and the metric names the one query to use and the ones that only look right.

metrics:
  - name: uninsured_deposits
    model: fct_bank_position
    expression: "sum(max(0, amount_per(insured_owner_key, ownership_category, bank_code) - 250000))"
    do_not_use:
      - "max(0, ledger_balance - 250000) per account: ignores the sweep and the owner"
      - "per holder after the sweep: double-counts the $250K for a sole proprietor with a personal account"
    known_gap: "deposits a customer holds directly at a program bank are invisible here"
